About Hiba

A young voice, carrying an old tradition

At just 13, Hiba Muzammil Qadri has already begun shaping a distinctive journey in Nasheed, Naat and spiritual Kalam. Beginning her journey at the age of 7–8, she has presented nearly 100 Nasheeds across Arabic, Urdu, English, Persian, Punjabi, Gujarati and Pashto, including all Six Kalimas in Arabic.

Her artistic journey has grown beyond solo performances, with collaborations alongside artists from the international music community, appearances in Ramadan live transmissions, and victories in school-level Naat competitions.

Her debut Kalam, "Pyari Maa," has surpassed 60 million views on another YouTube channel, while multiple works from her musical journey have achieved multi-million-view milestones. What sets Hiba apart is the character of her voice — naturally bright, sweet and melodious, enriched with soulful depth and spiritual warmth, carrying a distinctive emotional quality that connects across languages and cultures.

Hiba has already touched millions through her voice — building a growing artistic presence with grace, purpose and a journey that is only beginning.
